Additional Tips for Choosing Blanket Size

  • Seasonal Consideration: Winter may require larger blankets for better coverage
  • Washing Convenience: Ensure the selected blanket size fits easily in your washing machine
  • Storage Space: Consider storage space to avoid selecting overly large blankets
  • Material Impact: Some materials may slightly shrink after washing
  • Personal Preference: Some prefer the security of being fully covered by a blanket, while others prefer looser coverage
