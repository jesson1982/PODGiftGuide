Global Shipping Information & Time Guide

Learn about our shipping policies, timeframes, and costs. This guide covers all important information for domestic and international shipping to help you plan accordingly

Reading Time: 6 minutes Level: Beginner Published: 2026-01-20

Short answer: personalized gifts need 2-5 business days of production time before shipping, because each item is printed on demand. We recommend ordering 10-14 days before your occasion to allow for production plus transit, and double-checking spelling and photo placement at checkout, since customized items usually cannot be returned. The answer is simple: order early and proof carefully.

Order Processing Time

Personalized custom products require time to manufacture, here's what you need to know about processing times:

Standard Processing Time

All custom products require time to be made as each item is specially customized according to your order:

  • Standard Processing Time: 3-5 business days
  • Complex Designs: May require an additional 1-2 business days
  • Bulk Orders: Processing time may be extended based on order quantity

Expedited Processing

If you need faster delivery, you can choose expedited processing service (additional fee), which can reduce processing time to 1-2 business days.

Holiday Impact

Please note that public holidays and weekends are not counted toward processing time. During major holidays, processing times may be extended.

Domestic Shipping

We offer nationwide shipping services. Here's detailed information about domestic shipping:

Shipping Options

  • Standard Shipping: 7-10 business days, free
  • Express Shipping: 3-5 business days, $15
  • Next Day Delivery: Next business day, $35

Coverage Area

Our domestic shipping network covers all provinces and autonomous regions in mainland China, including:

  • Main cities (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, etc.)
  • Second-tier cities (Hangzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan, etc.)
  • County-level cities and remote areas

Shipping Limitations

Due to logistics limitations, some remote areas may require additional shipping time. We will notify you of specific estimated delivery times when confirming your order.

International Shipping

We proudly offer personalized custom gift shipping services to customers worldwide:

Shipping Regions

We currently provide shipping services to the following regions:

  • Asia: Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, India, etc.
  • North America: United States, Canada, Mexico
  • Europe: United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, etc.
  • Oceania: Australia, New Zealand
  • Other Regions: Based on logistics partner availability

International Shipping Times

  • Asian Region: 10-15 business days
  • North American Region: 12-20 business days
  • European Region: 12-18 business days
  • Oceania Region: 15-20 business days

Duties and Taxes

Please note that importing countries may charge duties and taxes, which need to be paid by the recipient. We recommend checking relevant policies in the destination country before placing your order.

Shipping Costs

Our shipping cost structure aims to provide fair and competitive pricing:

Domestic Shipping Costs

  • Standard Shipping: Free (order amount over $100)
  • Standard Shipping: $8 (order amount under $100)
  • Express Shipping: $15
  • Next Day Delivery: $35

International Shipping Costs

  • Asian Region: $45-65
  • North American Region: $65-85
  • European Region: $55-75
  • Oceania Region: $70-90

Bulk Order Discounts

For bulk orders (10+ items), we offer shipping cost discounts. Please contact customer service for quotes.

Package Tracking

We provide complete package tracking services so you can always know your order status:

Tracking Information Access

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can track your package through the following methods:

  • Visit our website and log into your account
  • Check real-time tracking information on the order details page
  • Use the tracking number in the email to query on carrier websites

Tracking Status Updates

Package tracking will show the following statuses:

  • Shipped: Package has left our warehouse
  • In Transit: Package is on its way
  • Out for Delivery: Package has reached destination city
  • Delivered: Package successfully delivered

Abnormal Situation Handling

If tracking information shows abnormalities (such as long delays), please contact our customer service team promptly.

Delivery Success Tips

Here are some suggestions to ensure smooth delivery:

Address Accuracy

Ensure the shipping address is accurate, including:

  • Complete street address (house number, building number, etc.)
  • Correct postal code
  • Contact phone number (for courier communication)
  • Recipient name (matching ID documents)

Delivery Preference Settings

  • Specify a secure package placement location
  • Provide an alternate shipping address
  • Choose weekday or weekend delivery
  • Specify adult signature required (if needed)

Special Delivery Requests

If you have special delivery requirements (such as specific delivery time), please indicate in the order notes. We'll do our best to fulfill your requests, but cannot guarantee fulfillment.

Summary

Our global shipping service aims to provide every customer with efficient, reliable, and convenient experience. From order processing to final delivery, we're committed to ensuring your custom gifts arrive safely and on time. If you have any questions about shipping, please contact our customer service team anytime.

Remember to consider sufficient shipping time when placing orders, especially during holiday seasons. For urgent orders, you can choose our expedited processing and express shipping services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a personalized gift take to arrive?

Production takes 2-5 business days because each item is printed on demand. Add transit time on top, and order 10-14 days before your occasion to be safe.

Can I change or cancel a custom order after checkout?

In most cases no, because production starts quickly on personalized items. Double-check spelling, dates, and photo placement before you confirm your order.

Do you ship personalized gifts internationally?

Yes. International delivery takes longer than domestic shipping and may involve customs processing, so place international custom orders at least three weeks ahead.
