How to Design the Perfect Custom Blanket

Learn how to create stunning custom blanket designs, from selecting the right images to adjusting proportions

Reading time: 8 minutes Level: Beginner Published: 2026-01-20

Short answer: designing a custom blanket comes down to five decisions: blanket size, material, photo quality, layout, and text personalization. We recommend starting with a high-resolution photo (at least 150 DPI at print size), choosing fleece for everyday use or sherpa for premium softness, and keeping the design to one strong image with a short message. In short, simplicity prints better than complexity.

Choosing the Right Image

The success of a custom blanket largely depends on the quality and relevance of the chosen image. Here are key factors to consider when selecting an image:

Image Types

The most popular image types for custom blankets include:

  • Family Photos: Family gatherings, vacations, or special occasions are popular choices for custom blankets
  • Pet Photos: Showcase your favorite pets and create heartwarming keepsakes
  • Artwork: Original paintings, digital art, or graphic designs
  • Text Messages: Quotes, commemorative messages, or personalized texts
  • Collage Designs: Combinations of multiple photos telling a complete story

Theme Considerations

Choose themes that align with the recipient's interests and personality. For example, select landscape photos for travel enthusiasts, or pet photos for animal lovers.

Image Quality Requirements

High-quality images are crucial for achieving clear print results. Here are important tips about image quality:

Resolution Requirements

To ensure clear images on blankets, it's recommended to use high-resolution original images:

  • Minimum resolution: 300 DPI (dots per inch)
  • For standard size blankets (150cm x 200cm), images should be at least 1770 x 2360 pixels
  • Higher resolution is better, providing greater flexibility

File Formats

Supported file formats include:

  • JPG/JPEG: Most commonly used format, suitable for photos
  • PNG: Supports transparent backgrounds, suitable for graphic designs
  • TIF/TIFF: High-quality lossless format, suitable for professional images

Avoiding Common Mistakes

Make sure to avoid these issues:

  • Using blurry or pixelated low-quality images
  • Downloading compressed images from social media
  • Screenshots or poorly scanned images

Design Layout Tips

Effective layouts can significantly enhance the visual appeal of custom blankets. Here are some layout tips:

Center Focus Layout

Place the main image in the center of the blanket with appropriate margins around it. This method is suitable for highlighting a single important image.

Repeated Pattern Layout

Arrange the same image repeatedly to create a continuous pattern effect. Perfect for small-sized images or icons.

Collage Layout

Combine multiple images to create a collage effect, telling a complete story. Pay attention to maintaining balance and harmony between images.

Borders and Decorations

Add borders, decorative elements, or text to increase the complexity and personalization of the design.

Color Selection Suggestions

Colors play an important role in custom blanket design, affecting overall visual effects and emotional expression:

Color Contrast

Ensure sufficient contrast between image colors and blanket background color to ensure the image is clearly visible. Light images suit dark blankets, and vice versa.

Emotional Colors

Consider the psychological effects of colors:

  • Warm tones (red, orange, yellow): Convey warmth, energy, and intimacy
  • Cool tones (blue, green, purple): Create calm and peaceful feelings
  • Neutral tones (black, white, gray): Classic, elegant, suitable for any environment

Seasonal Considerations

Select appropriate color palettes based on the season of use. For example, warm tones for winter, refreshing tones for summer.

Printing Considerations

Understanding the printing process helps achieve the best results:

Color Accuracy

Due to differences in printing technology and materials, screen colors may differ slightly from the final product. Consider ordering samples to check color accuracy.

Image Scaling

Custom services usually automatically adjust image size to fit the selected blanket size. Understand how this process affects your design.

Special Effects

Certain services offer special printing effects such as glow, texture, or dimensionality that can enhance the visual appeal of your design.

Preview Features

Use preview features provided by custom platforms to confirm how your design will look on the actual blanket.

Summary

Designing the perfect custom blanket requires careful consideration of image selection, quality, layout, and colors. By following the suggestions in this guide, you can create a blanket that is both beautiful and personally meaningful. Remember, the best design is one that resonates emotionally, so choose images and design elements that have special meaning to you and the recipient.

Before submitting your design, be sure to check all details, ensuring good image quality, reasonable layout, and harmonious color coordination. If in doubt, don't hesitate to contact the custom service provider's professional team for advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best images for custom blankets?

The best images for custom blankets include high-resolution family photos, pet photos, original artwork, meaningful quotes, or collages that tell a story. Ensure images are at least 300 DPI for optimal quality.

How do I ensure good image quality for printing?

For optimal printing quality, use high-resolution images (at least 300 DPI), with minimum dimensions of 1770 x 2360 pixels for standard blankets (150cm x 200cm). Save images in JPG, PNG, or TIFF formats.

What design layouts work best for custom blankets?

Popular design layouts include Center Focus (highlighting one main image), Repeated Pattern (creating continuous effects with small images), Collage Layout (combining multiple images), and designs with Borders and Decorations for added personalization.
