How to Keep a Custom Mug Print Flawless for Years: Care Guide

Published September 3, 2026 | 6 min read

A care specialist's practical routine for keeping printed and personalized products looking the way they did on day one.

Short answer: hand wash your custom mug with warm water, mild soap, and a soft sponge, then dry it with a cloth instead of air drying. Dishwashers expose the print to high heat, abrasive detergent, and chips from other dishes, which together fade most mug designs within months. Here's the routine I give every client: gentle wash, no soaking, and storage with a soft divider between mugs.

What Actually Damages Mug Prints

I have restored or assessed hundreds of personalized mugs, and the damage always traces back to a short list. Dishwasher heat and detergent strip the coating that protects sublimation ink. Scouring pads act like sandpaper on the print. Microwaving heats the printed area unevenly and degrades the coating over repeated sessions. Stacking mugs scuffs print against ceramic. None of these are dramatic on their own; together they destroy a design in months.

The Daily Care Routine

It takes about thirty seconds after you finish your drink:

  • Rinse the mug promptly so coffee and tea never set into stains
  • Wash with warm water, mild dish soap, and a soft sponge
  • Pay special attention to the rim and handle, where grime collects but prints usually are not
  • Towel dry with a soft cloth rather than leaving it on a wet dish rack

Common Habits and Their Impact on Mug Prints

Habit Impact on the Print Safe or Risky
Hand wash, soft sponge No measurable wear Safe
Dishwasher, top rack, gentle cycle Slow dulling over weeks Risky
Dishwasher, normal cycle Visible fading within one to two months Never
Scouring pad on the design Immediate scratching of the coating Never
Microwave, occasional use Gradual coating degradation Risky
Stacked storage, print touching ceramic Scuffing and chipping of the design Risky

Deep Cleaning Without Damage

For tea or coffee stains that a gentle wash will not lift, soak the mug in warm water with a spoon of baking soda for an hour, then wipe with a soft cloth. Baking soda is mildly abrasive, so let it do the work in the soak rather than scrubbing. Avoid lemon-based cleaners and bleach entirely; both attack the printed coating. Never put a printed mug through a bottle-sterilizing hot cycle.

Storage Tips From a Professional

Store mugs with space between them, or hang them on hooks by the handle. If cupboard space forces stacking, place a paper coaster or soft cloth between the printed area and the mug above it. Keep printed mugs away from the cupboard door hinge side, where vibration and knocks concentrate. These small habits are the difference between a mug that looks new in year three and one that looks tired in month three.

When to Re-Order

Even with perfect care, a daily-use mug eventually shows its age at the rim and handle first. When the print dulls despite careful washing, that is the coating giving up, not dirt. Re-ordering with the same seller is usually straightforward since designs are saved to your account. Frequently Asked Questions

Can custom mugs go in the dishwasher?

Only if the seller explicitly states the mug has a dishwasher-safe coating, and even then top rack with a gentle cycle is the limit. Standard custom mugs should be hand washed.

How long do custom mug prints last?

A quality sublimation print on a good ceramic mug stays vibrant for years of daily hand washing. Dishwasher use can dull the same print within one to two months.

Can I microwave a custom mug?

Occasional microwaving is usually fine, but repeated sessions degrade the protective coating over the print. If the mug is for daily hot drinks, a kettle and hand washing are kinder.

How do I remove coffee stains from the print area?

Soak the mug in warm water with a spoon of baking soda for about an hour, then wipe gently with a soft cloth. Never scrub printed areas with scouring pads or bleach cleaners.
