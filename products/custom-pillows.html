Personalized Custom Pillows

Our custom pillows are not only practical but also meaningful. Whether featuring your favorite pet's photo, family portraits, or your own artwork, these pillows add personality to your home. Soft fabrics and comfortable filling ensure the best user experience.

Each pillow is individually made to ensure the highest quality standards. We use advanced printing technology to ensure patterns remain vibrant and won't fade or peel even after multiple washes.

Usage Scenarios

  • Living room decor: Add personalized elements to your sofa area
  • Bedroom accents: Unique designs enhance room atmosphere
  • Gift giving: Perfect birthday, holiday, or anniversary gifts
  • Commercial use: Corporate events or promotional gifts

Specifications

  • Sizes: 16×16, 18×18, 20×20, 24×16 inches
  • Materials: Cotton, Linen, Polyester, Velvet
  • Filling: Polyester, Feather, Memory Foam
  • Printing: Full-color digital printing
  • Closure: Hidden zipper for easy removal
  • Care: Machine wash cold, low heat dry

Care Instructions

To extend the life of your custom pillow, follow these care guidelines:

  • Before first use, remove pillow insert and wash pillowcase separately
  • Use mild detergent and wash in cold water (max 30°C) on gentle cycle
  • Do not use bleach or chlorine-based cleaning products
  • Low heat dry or air dry, avoid direct sunlight
  • If ironing is necessary, use low heat (under 110°C), avoiding direct contact with printed surfaces
  • Regularly fluff and rotate pillow to maintain shape and elasticity
