Care Instructions

To extend the life of your custom mug, please follow these care guidelines:

  • Before first use, wash with warm water and mild detergent
  • Safe for microwave heating and dishwasher cleaning
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes to prevent cracking
  • Avoid using steel wool or other abrasive cleaning tools
  • When not in use for extended periods, store in a dry, ventilated place
  • If hand washing, use soft cloth and mild detergent
